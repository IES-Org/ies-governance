# Current Voting Members

This document records the current Voting Members of the IES Steering Group.

Voting Members hold formal decision-making rights within the Steering Group. Each active Domain Working Group has two Voting Member positions:

* the Domain Working Group Chair
* one Domain Working Group Representative

Domain Working Group Chairs and Domain Working Group Representatives are Voting Members of the Steering Group for as long as they hold those roles.

## Notes

* Each Domain Working Group Chair is a Voting Member of the Steering Group.
* Each Domain Working Group Representative is a Voting Member of the Steering Group.
* Each Voting Member has one vote.
* A Domain Working Group Chair may replace their Domain Working Group Representative at any point.
* Changes to Voting Members should be reflected in this document and, where relevant, in the Steering Group records.
* Where a vote is tied, the Steering Group Chair has the casting vote.
