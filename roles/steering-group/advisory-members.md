# Current Advisory Members

This document records the current Advisory Members of the IES Steering Group.

Advisory Members contribute specialist technical expertise and advice to the Steering Group. They participate in Steering Group discussions but do not hold voting rights.

The Steering Group may appoint up to two Advisory Members. Advisory Members must be members of an active Domain Working Group and should have strong technical expertise in ontology development, IES Common, and domain-driven extensions.

Both Advisory Member positions cannot be filled by people from the same Domain Working Group.
