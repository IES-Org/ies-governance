## Notes

* This document records past Common Ontology Maintainers only.
* Current Common Ontology Maintainers are recorded in [Current Common Ontology Maintainers](./current-common-ontology-maintainers.md).
* Common Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.
* Common Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top and IES Core.
* The Common Ontology Maintainer group must include at least one public sector representative.
* Changes to past maintainer records should be made when a Common Ontology Maintainer leaves the role.
