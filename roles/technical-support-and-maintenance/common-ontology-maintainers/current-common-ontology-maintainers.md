# Current Common Ontology Maintainers

This document records the current Common Ontology Maintainers for IES.

Common Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top and IES Core. They provide technical stewardship of the shared foundation used across domain-driven development in IES.

Common Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.

## Notes

* Common Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top and IES Core.
* Common Ontology Maintainers share collective responsibility for IES Top and IES Core.
* Individual maintainers may take lead responsibility for particular areas of expertise, but lead responsibilities do not create exclusive ownership of any part of IES Top or IES Core.
* Common Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.
* Changes to Common Ontology Maintainers must be reflected in this document and, where relevant, in the Steering Group records.
