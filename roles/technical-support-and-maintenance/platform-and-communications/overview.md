# Platform and Communications Overview

Platform and Communications Management supports the operation of the IES GitHub organisation and communication around IES governance, development, and release activity.

This function includes the GitHub Organisation Custodian role and any supporting communications responsibilities needed to keep IES accessible, navigable, and appropriately managed.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of Platform and Communications Management is to support the practical operation, accessibility, and visibility of IES.

This includes supporting the IES GitHub organisation, maintaining appropriate platform arrangements, and helping ensure that governance, development, and release information can be accessed and understood by contributors and users.

---

## Scope of Responsibility

Platform and Communications Management may include responsibility for:

* supporting the operation of the IES GitHub organisation
* managing organisation-level GitHub settings where authorised
* supporting repository creation, naming, visibility, and archiving arrangements
* supporting access and permission arrangements
* maintaining navigability and consistency across public-facing repositories
* supporting publication and release communications
* supporting governance documentation updates where relevant
* helping users and contributors find the appropriate repository, process, or contact route

This function supports the management of the platform and associated communications. It does not replace the responsibilities of Domain Working Groups, Common Ontology Maintainers, Repository Maintainers, or the Steering Group.

---

## GitHub Organisation Custodian

The GitHub Organisation Custodian is responsible for supporting the administration and integrity of the IES GitHub organisation.

This may include:

* supporting repository creation and configuration
* managing or supporting access permissions
* maintaining organisation-level settings where authorised
* supporting branch protection and repository protection settings
* helping ensure repository structures are consistent with IES governance
* supporting repository visibility and archiving arrangements
* coordinating with relevant repository maintainers where platform changes are required

The GitHub Organisation Custodian role is administrative and enabling. It does not confer authority to approve ontology changes, governance changes, or domain-driven development decisions.

---

## Communications Responsibilities

Platform and Communications Management may also support communication relating to IES governance, development, and release activity.

This may include:

* supporting public-facing information about IES
* helping maintain clear navigation between governance documents and repositories
* supporting publication of release information
* supporting communication of governance updates
* helping ensure that repository descriptions and documentation are clear and consistent

Communications activity should reflect approved IES governance documentation, approved releases, and authoritative repository content.

---

## Relationship with the Steering Group

The Steering Group provides strategic oversight and governance direction for IES.

Platform and Communications Management supports the Steering Group by helping ensure that the IES GitHub organisation and public-facing governance information remain accessible, coherent, and appropriately managed.

Issues relating to governance authority, strategic direction, public release approval, or material changes to governance arrangements must be escalated to the Steering Group where appropriate.

---

## Relationship with Domain Working Groups

Domain Working Groups lead domain-driven development within IES.

Platform and Communications Management may support Domain Working Groups by helping set up repositories, maintain consistent repository structures, support access arrangements, and ensure that domain-driven repositories are navigable within the IES GitHub organisation.

Domain Working Groups remain responsible for the domain substance of their work.

---

## Relationship with Common Ontology Maintainers

Common Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top and IES Core.

Platform and Communications Management may support the Common Ontology Maintainers by helping maintain appropriate repository settings, access arrangements, branch protections, and release-related platform arrangements.

Platform administration does not replace the technical authority or responsibilities of the Common Ontology Maintainers.

---

## Relationship with Repository Maintainers

Repository Maintainers are responsible for maintaining specific IES repositories.

Platform and Communications Management may support Repository Maintainers with GitHub organisation-level issues, repository settings, permissions, visibility, archiving, and consistency with mandatory repository structures.

Repository Maintainer information is maintained in the relevant repository’s maintainer file, not centrally in this governance repository.

---

## Access and Permissions

Platform and Communications Management may require elevated access to the IES GitHub organisation or specific repositories.

Access must be proportionate to the role being performed and must be managed in accordance with relevant security, governance, and repository-level requirements.

People performing Platform and Communications responsibilities must understand the implications of the permissions they hold, including organisation settings, repository settings, branch protection, access management, and repository visibility.

Access must be reviewed when a person leaves the role, no longer requires access, or where there is a relevant governance, security, or operational reason to review permissions.

---

## Limits of Authority

Platform and Communications Management is an enabling function.

It does not grant authority to:

* approve changes to IES Top or IES Core
* approve domain-driven ontology changes
* approve public releases
* change governance arrangements without the appropriate governance approval
* override repository-level security, assurance, or access requirements
* act on behalf of a Domain Working Group, Repository Maintainer, Common Ontology Maintainer, or the Steering Group unless authorised to do so

Where platform or communications activity depends on a governance decision, that decision must be made through the appropriate governance route.

---

## Escalation

Platform and communications issues should be resolved at the lowest appropriate level.

Escalation may be required where:

* access or permissions create governance or security concerns
* repository configuration affects governance, release, or technical integrity
* a repository visibility or archiving decision has wider implications
* communications activity requires governance approval
* an issue affects the integrity, availability, or accessibility of IES materials
* a matter requires Steering Group decision

Escalated matters should be taken to the relevant governance group through the appropriate route.
