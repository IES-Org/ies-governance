## Notes

* This document records past Platform and Communications role-holders only.
* Current Platform and Communications role-holders are recorded in [Current Platform and Communications Team](./current-platform-and-communications-team.md).
* Platform and Communications role-holders are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.
* The GitHub Organisation Custodian is the primary role-holder for GitHub organisation administration.
* Changes to past role-holder records should be made when a Platform and Communications role-holder leaves the role or changes role.
* Where a change in role-holder affected operational funding or continuity arrangements, the relevant record should be updated.
