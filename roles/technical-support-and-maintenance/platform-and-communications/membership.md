# Platform and Communications Membership

This document defines the membership model for Platform and Communications Management.

It describes who may hold Platform and Communications responsibilities, how role-holders are appointed, the expectations that apply to the role, and how current and past role-holders are recorded.

---

## Membership Model

Platform and Communications Management supports the operation of the IES GitHub organisation and communication around IES governance, development, and release activity.

This function includes:

* the GitHub Organisation Custodian
* any supporting Platform and Communications role-holders

The GitHub Organisation Custodian is the primary role-holder for GitHub organisation administration. Additional role-holders may support platform management, communications, documentation, navigation, or release communication activity where required.

Platform and Communications role-holders are appointed to support the practical management, accessibility, and visibility of IES. They are expected to act in the interests of IES as a whole and in accordance with the IES Charter.

---

## Eligibility

To hold a Platform and Communications role, an individual must have the judgement, reliability, and practical capability required to manage or support the IES GitHub organisation and associated communications activity.

A Platform and Communications role-holder should have a strong understanding of:

* GitHub organisation management
* repository settings and visibility
* repository access controls and permissions
* branch protection and repository protection settings
* issue and pull request workflows
* repository naming and mandatory repository structures
* governance documentation and repository navigation
* release communication and public-facing documentation
* operational requirements associated with GitHub organisation, website, domain, and publication infrastructure
* the distinction between platform administration, governance authority, and technical ontology maintenance

The GitHub Organisation Custodian must have sufficient GitHub capability to exercise elevated permissions responsibly.

Platform and Communications Management must be associated with at least one member of the IES initiative that has the ability to support or secure the operational funding required for the function.

---

## Appointment

Platform and Communications role-holders are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.

A candidate may be proposed where there is a clear need for platform administration, communications, documentation, or release communication capacity and where the candidate has the required practical capability.

Appointments should consider both the suitability of the individual candidate and the access, permissions, and operational funding responsibilities required to perform the role.

The appointment should be documented in the relevant Steering Group records and reflected in [Current Platform and Communications Team](./current-platform-and-communications-team.md).

---

## Number and Composition of Role-Holders

The number of Platform and Communications role-holders should remain proportionate to the needs of IES.

The function should have enough capacity to provide resilience, continuity, and effective support for the IES GitHub organisation and public-facing communications, but not so many role-holders that accountability or access control is weakened.

Where elevated GitHub permissions are required, the number of people holding those permissions must be limited to those who need them.

Platform and Communications Management must include, or be supported by, an IES initiative member with the ability to support or secure funding for the operational costs associated with the function.

---

## Responsibilities of Role-Holders

Platform and Communications role-holders are expected to:

* support the operation of the IES GitHub organisation
* manage or support organisation-level GitHub settings where authorised
* support repository creation, naming, visibility, and archiving arrangements
* support access and permission arrangements
* help maintain repository structures that are consistent with IES governance
* support branch protection and repository protection settings where authorised
* help maintain clear navigation across governance documentation and repositories
* support publication and release communications
* support management of operational dependencies such as GitHub organisation costs, website management, domain acquisition, domain renewal, and related publication infrastructure
* help identify, secure, or maintain the funding required for relevant platform and communications costs
* help users and contributors find the appropriate repository, process, or contact route
* escalate governance, security, release, technical, or operational sustainability matters through the appropriate route

Platform and Communications role-holders must act in accordance with the IES Charter and relevant governance, security, repository, and development processes.

---

## Funding and Operational Sustainability

Platform and Communications Management is responsible for identifying and managing the operational dependencies required to keep IES accessible and appropriately maintained.

This may include costs or commitments associated with:

* GitHub organisation services
* website management
* domain acquisition and renewal
* publication infrastructure
* communications tooling or services
* other platform or access arrangements needed to support IES

At least one organisation or member associated with Platform and Communications Management must have the ability to support, secure, or coordinate funding for these operational dependencies.

If funding, access to funding, or operational continuity is at risk, the Platform and Communications role-holders must escalate the issue to the Steering Group as a matter of urgency.

---

## Authority and Limits

Platform and Communications role-holders have authority to perform platform and communications tasks within the limits of the IES governance framework and the permissions granted to them.

The role is administrative and enabling. It does not confer authority to:

* approve changes to IES Top or IES Core
* approve domain-driven ontology changes
* approve public releases
* approve changes to governance arrangements
* override repository-level security, assurance, or access requirements
* act on behalf of a Domain Working Group, Repository Maintainer, Common Ontology Maintainer, or the Steering Group unless authorised to do so

Where a platform or communications task depends on a governance decision, that decision must be made through the appropriate governance route.

---

## Access and Permissions

Platform and Communications role-holders may require elevated access to the IES GitHub organisation or specific repositories.

Access must be proportionate to the role being performed and must be consistent with relevant security, governance, and repository-level requirements.

People holding elevated permissions must understand the implications of those permissions, including organisation settings, repository settings, branch protection, access management, repository visibility, and protected branches.

Access must be reviewed when a role-holder leaves the role, no longer requires access, changes responsibilities, or where there is a relevant governance, security, or operational reason to review permissions.

---

## Participation Expectations

Platform and Communications role-holders are expected to participate actively and constructively.

They should:

* respond to relevant platform or communications requests in a timely manner
* maintain appropriate records of material platform or communications changes
* use GitHub effectively and responsibly
* follow agreed repository naming, visibility, access, and archiving practices
* distinguish clearly between administrative action and governance approval
* monitor operational dependencies and escalate funding or continuity risks where required
* declare relevant conflicts of interest
* support transparent and traceable management of IES materials

---

## Conflicts of Interest

Platform and Communications role-holders should declare any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest in relation to matters under consideration.

A conflict of interest does not necessarily prevent participation, but it must be made transparent and managed appropriately. Where necessary, the relevant governance route may determine that a role-holder should not participate in a particular decision or administrative action.

---

## Review of Membership

Platform and Communications membership should be reviewed periodically to ensure that the function remains accurate, proportionate, and fit for purpose.

A review may consider:

* whether there is sufficient platform and communications capacity
* whether the current role-holders continue to meet the expectations of the role
* whether access and permissions remain appropriate
* whether elevated permissions remain necessary
* whether operational funding arrangements remain adequate
* whether any operational continuity risks need to be escalated
* whether additional appointments are required
* whether any role-holder should step down or be replaced

Any material change to Platform and Communications membership must be recorded in the relevant public records.

---

## Removal or Replacement

A Platform and Communications role-holder may step down from the role at any point.

A Platform and Communications role-holder may be removed or replaced by majority vote of the Steering Group where they:

* are no longer able to perform the role
* no longer require the role
* no longer meet the expectations of the role
* no longer require the access or permissions associated with the role
* are unable to support or coordinate required operational funding where that responsibility is attached to their role
* are unable to participate for a sustained period
* have a conflict of interest that cannot be managed appropriately
* materially fail to act in accordance with the IES Charter or governance processes

Access and permissions must be reviewed when a Platform and Communications role-holder leaves the role.

Where removal, replacement, or resignation would create a risk to operational funding or continuity, the issue must be escalated to the Steering Group.

---

## Public Record

Current and past Platform and Communications role-holders are recorded centrally in this governance repository.

Current role-holders are recorded in:

* [Current Platform and Communications Team](./current-platform-and-communications-team.md)

Past role-holders are recorded in:

* [Past Platform and Communications Team](./past-platform-and-communications-team.md)

The public record supports transparency, accountability, and continuity of platform and communications management.
