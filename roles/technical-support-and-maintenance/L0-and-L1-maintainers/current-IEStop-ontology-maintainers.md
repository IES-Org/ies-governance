# Current IES Top Ontology Maintainers

This document records the current IES Top Ontology Maintainers for IES.

IES Top Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top (Layer 0). They provide technical stewardship of the foundational ontology layer on which IES Core and the IES domain extensions depend.

IES Top Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.

## Notes

* IES Top Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top.
* Maintainers appointed to IES Top share responsibility for its technical integrity and maintenance.
* Individual maintainers may take lead responsibility for particular areas of expertise, but this does not create exclusive ownership of any part of IES Top.
* Appointment as an IES Top Ontology Maintainer does not confer responsibility, authority or repository permissions for IES Core or any domain extension.
* IES Top Ontology Maintainers must carefully consider the downstream effects of proposed changes on IES Core and IES domain extensions.
* Where specialist assessment is required, maintainers should engage with the relevant IES Core Ontology Maintainers or domain-managed technical teams.
* IES Top Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.
* Changes to IES Top Ontology Maintainers must be reflected in this document and, where relevant, in the Steering Group records.
