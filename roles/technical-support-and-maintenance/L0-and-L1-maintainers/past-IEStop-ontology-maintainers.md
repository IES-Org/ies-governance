## Notes

* This document records past IES Top Ontology Maintainers only.
* Current IES Top Ontology Maintainers are recorded in **Current IES Top Ontology Maintainers**.
* IES Top Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.
* IES Top Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Top.
* Appointment as an IES Top Ontology Maintainer does not confer responsibility or authority for IES Core or any domain extension.
* Changes to this record should be made when an IES Top Ontology Maintainer leaves the role.
* Where an individual continues to serve as an IES Core Ontology Maintainer, their departure from the IES Top role should still be recorded here separately.
