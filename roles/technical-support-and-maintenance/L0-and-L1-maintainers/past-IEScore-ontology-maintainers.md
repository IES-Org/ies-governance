## Notes

* This document records past IES Core Ontology Maintainers only.
* Current IES Core Ontology Maintainers are recorded in **Current IES Core Ontology Maintainers**.
* IES Core Ontology Maintainers are appointed by majority vote of the Steering Group.
* IES Core Ontology Maintainers are responsible for maintaining IES Core.
* Appointment as an IES Core Ontology Maintainer does not confer responsibility or authority for IES Top or any domain extension.
* Changes to this record should be made when an IES Core Ontology Maintainer leaves the role.
* Where an individual continues to serve as an IES Top Ontology Maintainer, their departure from the IES Core role should still be recorded here separately.
