# Domain Working Group Membership

This document defines the membership model for IES Domain Working Groups.

It describes how Domain Working Groups are formed, who may participate, the roles within a Domain Working Group, and what membership information is published in the governance repository.

---

## Membership Model

Domain Working Groups are communities of domain experts and contributors who support domain-driven development within IES.

Each Domain Working Group should have:

* a Domain Working Group Chair
* a Domain Working Group Representative
* Domain Working Group members

The Chair and Representative are official role-holders. They are published in this governance repository because they hold recognised governance roles and participate in the wider IES governance structure.

Full Domain Working Group membership is not published centrally in this governance repository.

---

## Participation

Domain Working Group participation is open to people and organisations with relevant interest, expertise, responsibility, or experience in the domain.

Participants may include people from:

* public sector organisations
* private sector organisations
* academia
* standards bodies
* professional communities
* other relevant stakeholder groups

Participation should support the purpose of the Domain Working Group and the development of IES in line with the IES Charter.

---

## Domain Working Group Chair

Each Domain Working Group should have a Chair.

The Chair leads the Domain Working Group and is responsible for supporting its effective operation. This includes coordinating meetings, supporting discussion, managing domain-level priorities, and ensuring that the Domain Working Group operates within the IES governance framework.

The Chair is a Voting Member of the Steering Group.

The appointment, replacement, or removal of a Chair is managed by the relevant Domain Working Group through its own agreed arrangements.

---

## Domain Working Group Representative

Each Domain Working Group Chair may appoint one Domain Working Group Representative.

The Representative supports the Domain Working Group’s participation in Steering Group activity and provides resilience where the Chair is unavailable or where complementary expertise is useful.

The Representative is a Voting Member of the Steering Group.

A Domain Working Group Chair may replace their Representative at any point.

---

## Domain Working Group Members

Domain Working Group members contribute subject matter expertise, review material, support development activity, and participate in discussions relevant to the domain.

Members do not become Steering Group members unless they are appointed as the Domain Working Group Representative or become the Domain Working Group Chair.

Domain Working Group membership does not automatically grant repository permissions. Repository permissions are managed separately through the relevant repository arrangements.

---

## Formation of a Domain Working Group

A Domain Working Group may be established where there is a clear domain need and sufficient participation to support meaningful domain-driven development.

The formation of a Domain Working Group should identify:

* the name of the Domain Working Group
* the domain scope
* the initial Chair
* the approach for appointing the Representative
* the intended repositories, development areas, or outputs
* any dependencies on IES Common
* any relationship with existing Domain Working Groups

Once established, the Domain Working Group should be recorded in [Active Domain Working Groups](./active-groups.md).

---

## Changes to Chair or Representative

Changes to the Chair or Representative should be reflected in the relevant public records.

Current official role-holders are recorded in:

* [Domain Working Group Official Role-Holders](./official-role-holders.md)

Where a new Chair is appointed, the Steering Group voting membership records should also be updated.

Where a Representative is appointed or replaced, the Steering Group voting membership records should also be updated.

---

## Publication of Membership Information

This governance repository publishes official Domain Working Group role-holders, not full Domain Working Group membership.

The public record includes:

* active Domain Working Groups
* Domain Working Group Chairs
* Domain Working Group Representatives

The public record does not include a complete list of Domain Working Group members.

This approach supports transparency for recognised governance roles while avoiding unnecessary publication of wider participant information.

---

## Participation Expectations

Domain Working Group participants are expected to contribute constructively and in line with the IES Charter.

Participants should:

* contribute relevant domain expertise
* support technically coherent domain-driven development
* consider alignment with IES Common
* review proposals and materials where appropriate
* declare relevant conflicts of interest
* respect the governance and development processes for IES

Domain Working Group Chairs and Representatives have additional responsibilities because they hold recognised governance roles and participate in the wider governance structure.

## Becoming a Member

People or organisations interested in contributing to a Domain Working Group should use the contact information provided for the relevant domain in the Ontology Index.

The Ontology Index identifies active domain areas, relevant repositories, responsible groups, and repository-level contact or maintainer information. This should be used as the first route for finding the appropriate Domain Working Group or repository contact.

Participation is managed by the relevant Domain Working Group and may depend on the scope of the group, the nature of the contribution, and the expertise or perspective being offered.

---

## Removal or Replacement

The removal or replacement of a Domain Working Group Chair is managed by the relevant Domain Working Group through its own agreed arrangements.

A Domain Working Group Representative may be replaced by the Domain Working Group Chair at any point.

Participation in a Domain Working Group may end where a participant chooses to leave, no longer has a relevant role or interest, is no longer able to participate, or materially fails to act in accordance with the IES Charter or relevant governance processes.

---

## Relationship with Repository Permissions

Membership of a Domain Working Group does not automatically grant access to edit or approve changes in an IES repository.

Repository permissions are managed at repository level. Each repository is expected to identify its maintainers and relevant access arrangements through its own repository-level documentation.

Where a Domain Working Group is responsible for a repository, the group should ensure that repository permissions and maintainer arrangements are consistent with IES governance and relevant development processes.
