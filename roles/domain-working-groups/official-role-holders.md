# Domain Working Group Official Role-Holders

This document records the official role-holders for active IES Domain Working Groups.

Only official role-holders are published in this governance repository. Full Domain Working Group membership is not published centrally.

Official role-holders include:

* Domain Working Group Chairs
* Domain Working Group Representatives

Domain Working Group Chairs and Domain Working Group Representatives are Voting Members of the IES Steering Group.

## Notes

* Each active Domain Working Group should have a Chair.
* Each Domain Working Group Chair may appoint one Domain Working Group Representative.
* A Domain Working Group Representative may be replaced by the relevant Domain Working Group Chair at any point.
* Domain Working Group Chairs and Domain Working Group Representatives are Voting Members of the Steering Group.
* Changes to official role-holders should be reflected in this document and, where relevant, in the Steering Group voting membership records.
